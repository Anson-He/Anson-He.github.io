My research centers on the security and robustness of modern artificial intelligence systems.

Adversarial Machine Learning
======

I study training-time poisoning, evasion risks, and the geometry of robust learning systems. Our AAAI 2026 work introduces a deferred poisoning attack that preserves apparently normal training and validation behavior while making the resulting model more vulnerable to small perturbations.

- [Deferred Poisoning paper](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i26.39318)
- [Deferred Poisoning code](https://github.com/Anson-He/Deferred-Poisoning-Attack)

AIGC Security
======

I investigate proactive protection against malicious image generation and editing. Our structure disruption work targets the self-attention mechanism of diffusion-based inpainting systems to protect sensitive image regions from harmful manipulation.

- [Structure Disruption preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19425)

LLM Security
======

I am interested in the reliability, attack surfaces, and trustworthy deployment of large language models and other foundation models.
